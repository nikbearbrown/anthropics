# Participation policy

Participation is what makes the group a group. It is tracked, not
graded, and the ledger is public to members.

## What counts

- **Reading the week's material before Thursday.** Self-reported. The
  reading is not extensive; the honesty of the self-report is.
- **Submitting the problem by Sunday, or by the deadline the
  late-submission policy allows.** A missing week counts against
  participation even if every other week is on time.
- **Attending Thursday.** Absences are fine; the group meets even
  when four people are out. Absences are not a failure to
  participate; a run of four consecutive absences is a signal to
  check in.
- **Convening once per term.** Every member takes a Thursday. The
  convenor picks the problem, opens the meeting, and writes the
  short note that becomes that week's meeting note.

## What does not count

- Correctness. The group is not scored on right answers. A wrong
  answer, presented honestly, is more valuable than a copied right
  one.
- Length. Neither a long submission nor a long talk earns a
  participation point.
- Volume in meeting. Talking a lot at meeting is not participation;
  it is sometimes the opposite.

## Ledger

The convenor updates the participation ledger in the shared folder
after each meeting. Corrections are welcome the following Sunday.
