# Late-submission policy

Adopted 2026-01-14 by unanimous vote of the study group. Revised 2026-03-02
(clarified the grace-hour language) and 2026-05-18 (added the seventh-day
floor).

## Rules

1. **Grace hour.** A submission counted as "on time" for any purpose is a
   submission recorded in the group's shared folder at or before the
   Sunday-evening deadline plus one full hour. Anything after the grace
   hour is late.
2. **First day late — 10% off.** For each submission that lands after the
   grace hour but before 23:59 on Monday (local time of the group), we
   subtract ten percentage points from the base score.
3. **Second day late — 20% off, cumulative.** By Tuesday's midnight, the
   penalty is twenty percentage points, applied to the base score, not on
   top of the day-one penalty.
4. **Days three through six — five percent per day, cumulative from the
   day-two figure.** Wednesday is twenty-five percent off. Thursday
   thirty. Friday thirty-five. Saturday forty.
5. **Seventh day and later — 50% floor.** No submission is ever penalised
   more than half its base score. The seventh-day cap is a floor, not an
   allowance to submit late; late work still fails the group's
   participation criterion, which is a separate ledger.

## Rounding

Fractional day-counts round **down**. A submission recorded at 27 hours
past the deadline is one day late for the purposes of this rule.
