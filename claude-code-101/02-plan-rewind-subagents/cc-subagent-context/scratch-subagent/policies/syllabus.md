# Autumn syllabus

The group's autumn-term reading. Twelve weeks. Each Thursday's convenor
picks one problem from that week's material and posts it by Monday.

1. **Week 1 — the pigeonhole principle.** Grimaldi §2.4. Warm-up: hats
   in a room.
2. **Week 2 — induction, done cleanly.** Rosen §5.1–5.2. The staircase
   argument, and one place it is wrong.
3. **Week 3 — counting: permutations and combinations.** Grimaldi §1.
   The password-length problem.
4. **Week 4 — probability, first exposure.** Ross §1–2. The birthday
   problem, worked twice.
5. **Week 5 — expected value.** Ross §3. St. Petersburg, argued fairly.
6. **Week 6 — graphs, first pass.** Bondy & Murty §1. Bridges of
   Königsberg, walked in person if the weather cooperates.
7. **Week 7 — trees.** Bondy & Murty §2. The minimum-spanning-tree
   proof, blackboard-only.
8. **Week 8 — algorithms: greedy.** CLRS ch. 15 opening. Interval
   scheduling.
9. **Week 9 — algorithms: divide and conquer.** CLRS ch. 4. Mergesort
   recursion, drawn to depth.
10. **Week 10 — dynamic programming, gently.** CLRS ch. 15. Rod cutting,
    coins, longest common subsequence.
11. **Week 11 — complexity: P and NP, informally.** Sipser ch. 7.
12. **Week 12 — a member's choice.** A topic voted on at meeting nine.

Late-submission rules apply to every weekly problem.
