# PROJECT.md — the intent layer (written by the human, first)

- Who is this for? Twelve people who already know each other and meet on Thursdays.
- What should they feel? That this took thirty seconds and asked nothing of them.
- What does this refuse? Accounts. Emails. Passwords. Anyone's data beyond a first name and a yes.
- What is done? `python3 check.py` passes, and the page shows the next Thursday's date computed in the browser.
- What is out of scope? Reminders, calendars, attendance history, anything that needs a server.
