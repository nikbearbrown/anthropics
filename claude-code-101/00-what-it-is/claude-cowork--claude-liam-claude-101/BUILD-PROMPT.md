# BUILD PROMPT — claude-101

## Episode concept
Most people who haven't tried Claude don't know where to start — they don't even know what surface to open or which plan to pick. This reel is a 93-second orientation: three surfaces (Browser / Desktop / Cowork), three plan tiers (free / pro / max), three vocabulary words (Token / Cowork / Code), five prompting rules, and the expert-mode combination of Skills plus Projects. A viewer who watches this reel once should be able to open Claude and know what they're looking at.

**B05 — ILLUSTRATION (five rules)**
