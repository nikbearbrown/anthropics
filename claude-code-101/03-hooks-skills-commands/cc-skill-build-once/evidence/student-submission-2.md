# Student submission — Priya S., short answer, week 4

**Prompt.** In one paragraph, explain why the median is more robust than the mean when a dataset contains outliers, and give one small numeric example.

**Answer.** Outliers move the mean a lot but move the median almost nothing, because the median is the middle after sorting and only depends on rank, not magnitude. So if a class of 5 people has quiz scores of 6, 6, 7, 8, and one person got a 1, the mean drops to 5.6 (looks bad for the class) but the median is still 6.
