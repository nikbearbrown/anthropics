# Student submission — Mira O., short answer, week 4

**Prompt.** In one paragraph, explain why the median is more robust than the mean when a dataset contains outliers, and give one small numeric example.

**Answer.** The mean adds every value and divides by how many there are, so one really big number pulls the whole thing up. The median just picks the middle after sorting, so it doesn't care how big the biggest number is. For example, on `[2, 3, 4, 5, 100]` the mean is 22.8 but the median is 4, and the 4 is a better summary of the group because most of the numbers are near it.
