# House style — exec-summary

## Voice

- First person plural only when the team owns the decision ("we
  kept the floor"). Otherwise third person, past tense for
  outcomes, present for state.
- No hedging. Do not use: "somewhat", "arguably", "it seems",
  "generally speaking", "in many cases". If a number is estimated,
  say so and give the range.
- Prefer verbs to noun phrases. "The classifier misroutes tickets"
  beats "The classifier is a source of ticket misrouting."

## Tense

- Outcomes: past. ("Backlog fell to 63 minutes.")
- Diagnoses: present. ("The floor holds paying tickets above medium.")
- Recommendations: imperative. ("Keep the classifier. Keep the floor.")

## Capitalisation

- No title case in headings after the first word (except proper nouns).
- Product names verbatim, including case. Team names roman.

## Forbidden words

- welcome, seamless, robust, leverage, synergy, delight, journey.
- Any of these is a fail — rewrite the sentence, do not comma-splice
  around it.

## Numbers

- Percentages: no space before %. ("94%", not "94 %".)
- Time: minutes as integers, hours as decimals to one place.
- Round only in the What section; keep exact numbers in Why and What's new.
