Chapter 4. Research. Knowing your market. The research plug-and-s is for anyone who needs to understand something thoroughly before making a decision. Competitive landscape. Market opportunity. Industry trends. Work needs. If your work involves synthesizing information from multiple sources into actionable understanding, this plug-and-excelerates the process significantly. You might not think of yourself as a researcher, but if you've ever spent an afternoon reading articles to understand a market before entering it or pulled together information about competitors before a pitch or tried to figure out whether an idea has legs, you've done the work this plug-and-supports. What IT does? Source synthesis. Give Cloud a topic in a set of sources, articles, reports, documents, and it identifies common themes, conflicting findings, and key takeaways. The output is synthesis, not summary. It doesn't just tell you what each source says. It tells you what they mean together. Creative intelligence. Understand how competitors position themselves, what they're building, how they communicate, and where the gaps are. This works with public information and becomes richer if you've collected competitor materials in your co-work folders. Literature and market review. For a given topic, Cloud finds relevant sources, evaluates them, and produces a structured overview of the current state of knowledge. This full when entering a new market, evaluating a technology, or preparing a strategy document. Gap analysis. After reviewing existing material on a topic, Cloud identifies what's been covered well and what's missing, either in your research or in the market. Citation management. When your research needs proper attribution, Cloud tracks sources and generates formatted citations. If you use a reference manager like Zotero, you can connect it. Getting started. Install the research plugin from the plugin sidebar. It works immediately for source synthesis and general research support. For academic or formal research, customize the citation format, APA, Chicago, MLA, and connect any reference management tools you use. For business research, customization might include specifying the industries and markets you care about most, so Cloud's research is focused rather than broad. Practical workflows. Market entry research. Before committing to a new market or product direction, I'm considering offering AI implementation consulting for accounting firms. Research this market. How big is it? Who's already doing this? What do the firms actually need and where are the opportunities? Cloud produces a structured briefing. Market context, existing players, common pain points, opportunity gaps, and considerations. You get in an hour what would take a day of reading and note-taking. Competitive deep dive. When you need to understand a specific competitor, compile everything publicly available about competitor. Your positioning, pricing model, target audience, recent product launches, and customer sentiment. I want to understand their strengths and weaknesses. Cloud gathers and synthesizes public information into an intelligence briefing. This becomes the foundation for your positioning strategy. Trend synthesis. When you've collected a batch of articles or reports on a topic, I've saved 15 articles about AI adoption and professional services to my research folder. Read through them and give me the three most consistent trends where the sources disagree and what this means for a consultancy like mine. This is synthesis, not what do the articles say, but what should I take away from them? The distinction matters enormously when you're using research to make decisions. Decision support. When you're weighing options, I'm choosing between two approaches from a next product launch, a free trial model or a freemium model. Research the advantages and disadvantages of each, specifically for B2B-SIS products, in the 50 to $200 per month range. Include examples of companies that have used each approach successfully. Cloud provides a structured comparison based on available evidence, helping you make an informed decision rather than going with your gut. Tips. Specify what you'll do with the research. Research AI trends produces a generic overview. Research AI trends so I can decide whether to add AI services to my consulting practice, produces focused, actionable intelligence. The purpose ships the output. This reference findings. Ask Cloud to note when sources contradict each other. Disagreement among experts is important information, not a problem to be resolved. Build a research habit. Set aside time weekly or monthly to use the research plugin for competitive monitoring and market awareness.
