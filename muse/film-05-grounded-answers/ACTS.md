# ACTS.md — Grounded Answers (Film 5 of 12)

**Exact title:** Grounded Answers

**Core promise:** By the end, the viewer knows when to turn on search
grounding and what it costs: the same question asked twice, what grounding
adds, what it can't reach, and the per-query price.

**Structure:** hook + key terms + three acts (8 body beats) + recap + your
turn + outro. 13 beats, ~282 s (~4m42s).

- **Hook (BIDEA):** what the model knows (training) vs what it can look up
  (search).
- **Key terms (BDEFS):** Grounding · Freshness · Cost per query · Blocked page.
- **Act 1 — The test (3 beats):** B01 the question asked twice (control arms
  for a Dodge Grand Caravan, bought in Canada); B02 the ungrounded answer
  (generic, no current facts); B03 the grounded answer (retailers, shipping,
  prices as they are now).
- **Act 2 — Reading the difference (3 beats):** B04 the two answers side by
  side; B05 the three things grounding added (prices, Canadian retailers,
  shipping reality); B06 the limit: blocked product pages the search can't
  reach.
- **Act 3 — The price (2 beats):** B07 grounding is billed per search, per
  thousand queries — not by the token; B08 the rule of thumb: ground when the
  answer changes with time, skip it when the question is timeless.
- **BVDT:** exactly 3 lines, one per act.
- **BHTF:** ask one question the model can't know from training — this
  week's news, a price right now — then ask it grounded, and compare.
- **BOUT:** "Muse, in for Bear. Thanks for watching." + teaser for Film 6
  (Structured Outputs).

**Tone:** teardown of a single experiment, like a side-by-side taste test.
Honest about the limit (blocked pages) and the price — this is a tool with a
cost, not a default.

**What this film is not:** not a claim about which retailers were best; not a
pricing database — the film states the mechanism (retailers, shipping,
prices), never specific stores or dollar figures, because those live in the
transcript, not in this package.

**Cast per act (one look):** the question is one white card with three chips
("control arms" / "Dodge Grand Caravan" / "Canada"), reused across Act 1;
answers are cards — greyed, stale rows for ungrounded; green-checked fresh
rows for grounded; blocked pages are a globe with a wall; price is coins
stacking on a meter; the when-to-ground rule is a toggle switch.

**Source facts:**
- The instructor asked the same question with and without grounding:
  control arms for a Dodge Grand Caravan, bought in Canada. (transcript §7)
- The grounded answer added retailers, shipping, and price reality.
  (transcript §7)
- Grounding can't penetrate blocked product pages. (transcript §7)
- Web-search grounding is billed per one thousand queries, not by the token.
  (transcript outline l.24; the playground has a grounded-search toggle,
  l.34)

- The exact per-1k dollar rate — not in the sources; the film says "per

- The rest of the playground controls — Film 2's subject; here only the
