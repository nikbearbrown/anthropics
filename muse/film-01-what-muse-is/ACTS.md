# ACTS.md — What Muse Is (Film 1 of 12)

**Exact title:** What Muse Is

**Core promise:** By the end, the viewer can name the three models, say what
each one is for, explain "vertical integration" in one sentence, and describe
how Muse is priced.

**Structure:** hook + key terms + four acts (11 body beats) + recap + your
turn + outro. 15 beats, ~300 s (~5 min).

- **Hook (BIDEA):** the question — what changes when one company builds the
  model *and* the tools?
- **Key terms (BDEFS):** Muse Spark · Muse Glimmer · Vertical integration ·
  Token pricing.
- **Act 1 — The bet (2 beats):** B01 vertical integration (own models + own
  harness); B02 why it matters (a new option in the EI space).
- **Act 2 — The models (4 beats):** B03 Muse Spark 1.2 (managed, multimodal);
  B04 Muse Glimmer (30B open weights, one GPU); B05 Llama in one line (the
  famous sibling, not this series' subject); B06 benchmarks (the "Goldilocks"
  reading — upper-middle, third-best at everything).
- **Act 3 — The price tag (3 beats):** B07 token pricing, no subscription;
  B08 the contributor tier (deep discount for sharing data, much lower rate
  limits); B09 what it costs in practice (API coding prices; $20–50/month of
  heavy use is realistic).
- **Act 4 — The honest picture (2 beats):** B10 Meta's bet (transaction fees,
  owning the intent layer); B11 who it's for and its honest limits.
- **BVDT:** one line per act (4 lines).
- **BHTF:** open the playground, set a spend limit, run one prompt.
- **BOUT:** "Muse, in for Bear. Thanks for watching." + teaser for Film 2
  (Your First Prompts).

**Tone:** plain-spoken explainer, curious, no hype. Claims only what the
sources prove; the instructor's colorful framings ("Goldilocks",
"third best at everything") are attributed as his characterization, not
measured fact.

**What this film is not:** not a tutorial (that's Films 2–11); not the
consumer-agent story (that's Film 12); not a benchmark deep-dive.

**Cast per act (one look):** the three models are cards — Spark = terracotta
card, Glimmer = blue card, Llama = grey card — reused everywhere they appear.
The dollar/price motif is a single coin glyph. The "one company" idea is two
boxes (models + harness) merging into one.

**Source facts:**
- Muse Spark 1.2 is the current managed checkpoint (1.1 earlier); multimodal:
  text, image, video, audio in and out. (transcript §2)
- Muse Glimmer: 30B parameters, open weights (not open source), runs on a
  single GPU; trained on 100+ languages. (transcript §2)
- Pricing: token usage, no subscription model. API coding pricing per the
  research brief: $1.25 per million input tokens, $4.25 per million output;
  $0.10 / $0.20 with data sharing. (brief §3; transcript §2 corroborates
  token-based, no-subscription, ~$1.25 in / ~$0.10 out with sharing)
- Contributor tier: deep discounts for sharing data; much lower rate limits.
  (transcript §2)
- No batch pricing at time of recording. (transcript §2 — flagged: may have
  changed; film says "at launch")
- REST API; OpenAI-compatible and Anthropic-messages-compatible; no
  first-party SDK by design. (transcript §2)
- Benchmarks: instructor's characterization — "Goldilocks", upper-middle of
  leaderboards, "third best at everything". (transcript §2 — attributed)
- Business model: transaction fees paid by merchants (affiliate-like);
  subscriptions secondary; strategic goal is owning the intent layer.
  (brief §4)
- In Nik's experience, Spark is noticeably less capable than GPT-6 Astra,
  Claude Fable, or Claude Opus. (brief §1 — judgment, attributed)
