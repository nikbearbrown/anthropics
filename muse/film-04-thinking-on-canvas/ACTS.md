# ACTS.md — Thinking on Canvas (Film 4 of 12)

**Exact title:** Thinking on Canvas

**Core promise:** By the end, the viewer can use Muse as a brainstorming
partner that draws its thinking: ask for ideas, judge them, ask for a diagram,
refine it — and know what to do when the tooling fights back.

**Structure:** hook + key terms + three acts (9 body beats) + recap + your
turn + outro. 14 beats, ~282 s (~4m42s).

- **Hook (BIDEA):** use the model as a brainstorming partner — one that draws.
- **Key terms (BDEFS):** Brainstorm · Mermaid · Mind map · Iteration.
- **Act 1 — Ask (3 beats):** B01 "make the plugins funner": vague ask →
  bullet list of ideas; B02 judging the ideas (some good, some confusing);
  B03 picking what to draw.
- **Act 2 — Draw (4 beats):** B04 ask for a mermaid mind map; B05 reading the
  map (learning, growth, belonging…); B06 outer branches into grounded,
  color-coded plugin examples; B07 when the editor fights back (the mermaid
  part, not the model).
- **Act 3 — Refine (2 beats):** B08 the loop: ask, draw, refine; B09 what this
  loop is for — thinking on canvas.
- **BVDT:** exactly 3 lines, one per act.
- **BHTF:** pick one idea you have, ask for three variations, then ask it to
  draw the best one.
- **BOUT:** "Muse, in for Bear. Thanks for watching." + teaser for Film 5
  (Grounded Answers).

**Tone:** workshop-like, curious, honest about friction. The film shows the
messy middle (confusing ideas, broken paste) as part of the method, not as
failure.

**What this film is not:** not a mermaid tutorial; not the website-build story
(that's Film 3); not a verdict on which ideas were "right".

**Cast per act (one look):** idea rows are white cards with dot bullets;
the mind-map is one tree (terracotta center node, blue branch nodes, green
leaf nodes, color-coded per branch in B06); the loop is three nodes with
cycling arrows, reused in B08 only.

**Source facts:**
- The instructor asked for "single point bullets" of community-engagement
  plugin ideas to review. (transcript §6)
- Returned ideas included: deploy confessions (anonymous prod stories),
  one-click coffee match, code review thunderdome, stump the stack, kudos
  terminal, project matching, live AMAs, mentor questionship. (transcript §6)
- He then asked for "a visual mind map of points of community engagement" as
  a mermaid diagram. (transcript §6)
- The map's branches covered learning & growth, experimentation,
  participation, identity & belonging, recognition. (transcript §6)
- He asked for two to three grounded plugin examples per outermost branch,
  each branch color-coded uniquely. (transcript §6)
- Pasting/rendering the mermaid failed repeatedly in his editor; he fixed it
  with a fresh folder and a preview plugin. His verdict: "The mermaid part,
  not the model. The model seems to have produced what I want." (transcript §6)
- Reasoning was on extra high for the brainstorm. (transcript §6)
