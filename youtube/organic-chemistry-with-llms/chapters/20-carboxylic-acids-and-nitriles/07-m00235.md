We commented earlier in this chapter that carboxylic acids are similar
in some respects to both alcohols and ketones. Like alcohols, carboxylic
acids can be deprotonated to give anions, which are good nucleophiles in
SN2 reactions. Like ketones, carboxylic acids undergo addition of
nucleophiles to the carbonyl group. However, carboxylic acids also
undergo other reactions characteristic of neither alcohols nor ketones.
