Alkynes, like alkenes, can be cleaved by reaction with powerful
oxidizing agents such as ozone or KMnO4, although the reaction is of
little value and it is mentioned only for completeness. A triple bond is
generally less reactive than a double bond, and yields of cleavage
products can be low. The products obtained from cleavage of an internal
alkyne are carboxylic acids; from a terminal alkyne, CO2 is formed as
one product.
