# BUILD PROMPT — claude-linkedin

## Episode concept
LinkedIn content is pattern-based — and patterns are exactly what Claude is good at reading. This reel shows viewers how to turn a pile of past posts into a trained writing assistant: extract public posts with Apify, upload the spreadsheet to Cowork, let Claude build a full analysis report, convert that report into a reusable /skill, and then invoke the skill whenever a new post is needed. The reel is honest about where this falls short: it needs enough posts to learn from, it cannot invent novelty, and it only performs as well as the content it was trained on.

## Beat-by-beat production notes

**B01 — COMPOSER_ASK (cold open)**
Namaste (Hindi/Sanskrit greeting). The ask frames the whole premise: can Claude learn your voice? Answer preview confirms yes, with Apify as the entry point.

**B02 — ILLUSTRATION: Five-step pipeline**
Draw-on animation. Vertical numbered list with a terracotta vertical spine on the left. Five nodes: extract / download / upload / report+skill / generate. Labels are short (3–4 words each). This beat establishes the full workflow before the detail beats unpack each step. No screenshots.

**B03 — SPARK: Apify free extraction.**
Short beat. The key point is that this is safe/legal (public data) and free to start. Dollar figures are stripped; the free-tier message is enough.

**B04 — SPARK: Report + SOP in one run.**
Covers the Cowork analysis step. The word "SOP" (standard operating procedure) is important — it positions the output as a repeatable system, not a one-time experiment.

**B05 — SPARK: One skill. Your voice.**
Covers skill creation. Emphasizes the team-sharing angle — this is not just a personal tool.

**B06 — SPARK: Patterns, not magic.**
Honest limitations beat. Three constraints: needs volume (post frequency), needs quality (bad training data = bad output), and repeats patterns (no novelty invention). This beat builds credibility.

**B07 — HANDOFF**
Concrete four-step action: Apify → spreadsheet → Cowork → skill. The "one session" framing sets realistic time expectations.

**B08 — OUTRO**
Closing line frames the human-AI collaboration: Claude learns patterns, viewer brings taste. Episode title in caps. Liam sign-off.
