# Green, Yellow, Red

**What this is.** A show-tell explainer (about 4.9 minutes) of how the NDA review skill in Anthropic's open Claude for Legal repo (commercial-legal plugin) triages inbound NDAs, drawn as a belt of pages running into a sorter with three chutes. The skill ships with no default positions: the team's playbook is written by the cold-start interview (its positions, escalation rules and deal-breaker, then five to ten signed agreements, where "the delta is the real playbook") into a plain-English CLAUDE.md practice profile with a sales side and a purchasing side. Each NDA first gets a scope check (obligations beyond confidentiality go to yellow automatically), then the playbook's checks. GREEN routes to signature when every position is satisfied; YELLOW surfaces each deviation or gap for a named approver; a silent playbook gets your position recorded; RED stops for legal first. GREEN is locked unless the positions are attorney-reviewed, and a non-lawyer is gated before signature. Every output is, in the repo's words, "a draft for attorney review, not legal advice."
