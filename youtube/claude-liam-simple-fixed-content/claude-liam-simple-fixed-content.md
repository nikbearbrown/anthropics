# Why an AI Can Change a Word You Chose on Purpose

Ask an AI to fix one typo in your résumé and it can quietly change a word you picked
deliberately. Not because its word is worse — often it's fine — but because nothing in
the system was stopping it. These models have no copy button: when you ask for the
whole paragraph back, every untouched word is re-predicted from scratch. Watermarking
can add a second push on top of that. The fix isn't a firmer prompt.

## YOUR TURN

Take something you wrote and care about. Ask for one specific fix — and ask for the
answer as a list of changes with locations, not as the rewritten text. Then diff it
against your original and count every word that moved.

The count is almost never zero, and the words that moved were never named in your
request. That gap is the whole argument, measured on your own writing.

## One flag, stated in the video

Whether a given watermark adds drift depends on its design. Some are built to leave
the odds untouched. The reel says so out loud rather than asserting that every
watermark distorts editing — that distinction is doing real work and it belongs on
screen, not in a footnote.
