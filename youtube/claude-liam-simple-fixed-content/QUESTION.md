# QUESTION

**The question:** "Why does an AI editor change words I never asked it to touch?"

**Asked by:** a writer, after handing a résumé to an assistant for a typo fix.

**Why it earns a reel:** the intuitive answer — it thought its word was better — is
wrong, and being wrong about it sends people to the prompt box for a fix that
doesn't exist there. The correct answer is architectural and it changes what you build.

**Host line (B00):**

> "Someone asked why an AI editor changes words it was never asked to touch. The
> answer isn't what you'd guess. Liam. Take them through it."
