# CARRY-OUT — GATE C

Written before any other narration. Every beat exists to make this sentence survivable.

## The line

> **The problem isn't that the model picked a worse word. It's that it was allowed
> to pick a word at all.**

## The wrong guess it defeats

That unrequested edits are a **quality** failure — the model judged your word and
preferred its own — and therefore that a firmer instruction ("change nothing else")
is the fix.

It is not a quality failure. It is an **authority** failure. The model was handed
discretion over text where no generation decision was required.

## The secondhand test

*If someone repeats only this sentence in a meeting next week, is it still true?*

Yes — and it changes what they build. It moves the fix from the prompt box to the
architecture, which is the whole point.

## What it deliberately does not say

- Not "AI editing is unreliable." It is reliable at what it was asked to do.
- Not "watermarking is bad." Watermarking is one route to the failure, not the cause.
- Not a summary of the argument — it compresses the **distinction**: worse word vs.
  unauthorized word.
