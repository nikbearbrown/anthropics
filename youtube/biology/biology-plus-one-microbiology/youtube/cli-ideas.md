PASS/FAIL displayed.
- **Prompt seed:** `claude "Show: Read CLAUDE.md and DESIGN.md. Conform to them. Say: Build 00-growth-curve.html — bacterial growth curve simulator using logistic growth dN/dt = r*N*(1-N/K) with Euler integration. Visually verify the four phases: flat lag, steep log, plateau stationary, optional decline.
