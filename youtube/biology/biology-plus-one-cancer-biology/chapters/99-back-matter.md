## About the Author

**Nik Bear Brown** holds a PhD in Computer Science from UCLA in computational and systems biology, with secondary fields in AI and statistics. He completed a part-time postdoc at Harvard Medical School in deep learning while teaching at Northeastern University, and held early-career positions at DNAX Research Institute and Cetus Corporation as an NSF IGERT Bioinformatics Fellow.

His co-authored paper *The Landscape of Nanomedical Clinical Trials* appeared in *Nano Today* (2026). His open-source textbook [*Cancer Biology and Therapeutics*](https://github.com/nikbearbrown/Cancer-Biology-and-Therapeutics) — of which this is the trade edition — is taught at Northeastern.

He works in Boston. [nikbearbrown.com](https://www.nikbearbrown.com) · [irreducibly.xyz](https://irreducibly.xyz) · [skepticism.ai](https://www.skepticism.ai)
