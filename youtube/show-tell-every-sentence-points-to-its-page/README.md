# Every Sentence Points to Its Page

**What this is.** A show-tell explainer (about 2.5 minutes) of citations in the Claude API, drawn as one cast. You put documents in the request next to your question and switch citations on for each one (all or none). Each document is cut into chunks: sentences for plain text and PDFs. Claude answers in text blocks, and every cited claim sends an ink thread back to its passage. What the thread points to depends on the document: a character range (from 0) for plain text, a page range (from 1) for a PDF, a block index (from 0) for custom content you chunk yourself. The cited text rides back free of output tokens, and the pointers are guaranteed valid. A line with no thread is Claude's own reasoning: the one to check.
