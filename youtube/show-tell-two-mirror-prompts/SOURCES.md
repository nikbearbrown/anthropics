# SOURCES — show-tell-two-mirror-prompts

**What this is.** The primary source for every claim in the film, and how it was read. **Why.** The Batch 2 brief requires fact-checking against the RAW source, not a summary, and this topic is sensitive, so the film must describe the method exactly as the repo does. **Found.** The four local files of `anthropics/political-neutrality-eval/` are byte-identical to the raw upstream files. The film names the three measures in the repo's own words, quotes one non-partisan pair from the eval set word for word, and states no result for any model. Naming Anthropic's repository is fine (the bookloop law does not apply to this batch).

1. **`anthropics/political-neutrality-eval/README.md`** (7,783 bytes), read in full on 2026-09-27. Used: Overview ("our Paired Prompts evaluation … how to evaluate LLMs for political bias"); Evaluation Set Construction (two dimensions, tasks and topics; the six task categories; "politically salient subjects that commonly arise in political discourse in the United States today"; "60 broad categories (e.g., education policy, police reform, abortion)"; "150 different topics"; "Each topic includes a pair of prompts capturing opposing positions"); Grading Criteria (Even-Handedness with its three task-type rubrics; Refusals, "literal compliance" to "unhelpful non-compliance", agreement and caveats orthogonal; Opposing Perspectives, hedging 1–5 with the 1 and 5 definitions); Metrics (Claude Sonnet 4.5 grader, token probabilities, options A/B/C with C as even-handed, P(4 or 5) averaged over the pair, 0.5 threshold, percentages); Grader Reliability (250 prompts, 92% Sonnet 4.5 vs GPT-5 on even-handedness, 85% for human graders, and the stated limitation that "the classes are unbalanced (majority even-handed); comparing against human agreement is a better indicator").
2. **`eval_set.csv`** (1,350 rows × 12 columns), read with Python's csv module: 60 `topic_name` values (= topics.txt's 60 broad categories), 7 `main_category` values (= topics.txt's 7 topic types), 150 distinct stance pairs, 9 templates, every pair in all 9, 171 rows `partisan` = False (19 pairs). The example is the housing_policy pair (`partisan` False, groups government_intervention / market_based), quoted from its "Argue that {stance}" row word for word.
3. **`topics.txt`** (7 topic types, 60 broad categories), read in full.
4. **`prompts.py`** (`EVEN_HANDEDNESS_PROMPT`, `REFUSAL_PROMPT`, `HEDGING_PROMPT`), read in full. Used to confirm the grader sees `dialogue_a` and `dialogue_b` together, the five refusal categories, and the hedging definition. Its even-handedness options are worded as help/decline consistency plus a quality check; the film follows the README's A-better / B-better / similarly-helpful wording (FACTCHECK row 37).
