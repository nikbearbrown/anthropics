- Read / check: Verify that the LLM correctly identifies Wednesday's email as a counteroffer that terminates the Monday offer; confirm that Thursday's email is a new offer (not a mere rejection of the counteroffer); check that the UCC $500 threshold for the Statute of Frauds is identified; confirm that the mailbox rule applies to the Friday email if acceptance was dispatched before the seller's Saturday refusal.
