# QUESTION — What's a prompt, really?

**Question verbatim:** What's a prompt, really?

**Source:** General audience — this is one of the most common first questions from people who've heard the advice "write a better prompt" but don't know what a prompt actually is.

**Name may be used:** N/A — no individual named.

**Why it matters:** Most people picture a prompt as the words they type into the chat box, like a search query or a voice command. The actual answer — that the prompt is the entire assembled block of text the model receives, including conversation history and any system instructions — changes how they think about both what Claude is reading and why the usual advice works.
