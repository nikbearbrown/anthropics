## B00 (cold open)
Kia ora, Bear. Computational skepticism runs this channel, including its own pipeline. The book's two exercises are build and audit, so you'll see both here — what this process automates, and where it gets checked. Here's what an AI-built video pipeline looks like when you're honest about which parts are automated.

## B01 (wrong model)
The wrong model: AI makes the videos now. The human reviews the finished product. That's not a pipeline — it's an abdication dressed as automation.

## B02 (right model)
The right model: a pipeline where the machine owns the deterministic work — conform, render, captions, quality checks — and a human owns the argument and signs before anything costs money.

## B03 (what's in the playlist)
Four things: script to beats to audio as master clock, why audio-first, where the gates go, and what quality control means when nobody watched the render.

## B04 (do now)
Find the step in your own production process that is purely mechanical and script that one first.

## B05 (verdict)
For makers who want throughput without shipping slop. This is this channel's own pipeline, shown with its seams exposed. Falsifiable line: if the automated QC in this pipeline catches fewer errors than a human watching the render would, the automation is theater.
