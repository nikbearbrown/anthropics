# Claude on Your Desk

**What this is.** A 151-second show-tell explainer of `anthropics/claude-desktop-buddy`, drawn on a desk: the Claude app on a laptop, a tiny ESP32 device beside it, and the opt-in Bluetooth link between them. It has labels only, and Liam's voice does the explaining. **Why.** Card #6 in `../show-tell-ideas.md`; Bear said to build all the cards in order (2026-09-26). **Found.** Every claim matches the repo's README, REFERENCE.md and CONTRIBUTING.md (and the firmware where it matters). Claude for macOS and Windows can connect Cowork and Claude Code sessions to a maker device over Bluetooth Low Energy. It's opt-in (the bridge is off by default; developer mode, then Hardware Buddy, then Connect), meant for makers, and not an officially supported product feature. The app sends a snapshot on every change and every 10 seconds (sessions running and waiting, a few recent transcript lines, and a permission prompt when one is pending). The device can answer that prompt: it echoes the prompt's ID with `"once"` (approve that one tool call) or `"deny"`. The team's example is a desk pet that sleeps, wakes when a session starts, gets impatient while a prompt waits (its light blinks), and shows hearts if you approve quickly. Because transcript snippets and tool hints cross the link, the reference says to require encrypted pairing with a six-digit passkey. You don't need the example code: REFERENCE.md is the contract (Nordic UART Service, one JSON line at a time), and the repo's advice is that the best contribution is a fork. Two overclaims were corrected before audio (see FACTCHECK): the example has three buttons, not two, and "not a gadget you buy" became the README's own "isn't a product feature".

- The beats: the desk (B00); off by default, you opt in (B01); the snapshot and its recent lines (B02); the pet sleeps, then wakes (B03); a Bash permission prompt mirrored onto the device (B04); approve with the front button, `once`, deny with the other, hearts (B05); encrypted pairing with a passkey (B06); REFERENCE.md as a blueprint anyone can build from, and a fork (B07). Your turn: paste REFERENCE.md into Claude and plan your own device (what it shows, which prompts it may approve with a button, what it must never do without a person), then check that every approval is a button press and that the field names match REFERENCE.md.
