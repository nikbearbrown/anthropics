# QUESTION

**The question:** "Is that weird AI word the watermark showing through?"

**Asked by:** a reader who'd just learned Claude's text is watermarked and started
noticing *delve* everywhere.

**Why it earns a reel:** the answer isn't "no" — it's "backwards." Repetition is
evidence *against* watermarking, and the reason why explains what a watermark actually
is better than a definition would. The error is also consequential: people are
grading essays on it.

**Host line (B00):**

> "Someone asked if that odd AI-sounding word is the watermark showing through.
