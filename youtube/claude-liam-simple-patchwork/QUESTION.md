# QUESTION

**The question:** "Which law made my AI images carry a hidden mark?"

**Asked by:** a reader outside both the EU and California — which is what makes the
question sharp.

**Why it earns a reel:** the assumed answer is "the EU," and for most people asking,
the real answer is "none of them." That gap matters, because "a law requires this"
and "a company decided this" have completely different implications for what changes
next and who you'd ask to change it.

**Host line (B00):**

> "Someone asked which law made their AI images carry a hidden mark. Turns out,
> probably none of them. Liam. Take them through it."
