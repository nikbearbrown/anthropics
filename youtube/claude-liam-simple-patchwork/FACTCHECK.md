# FACTCHECK — claude-liam-simple-patchwork

**STATUS: NEEDS PRIMARY-SOURCE VERIFICATION BEFORE PUBLISH.** Not before render.

Every factual claim in this reel is a legal claim about currently-effective law, drawn
from two research documents supplied by Bear. Two things follow:

1. **These are not first-party records.** Unlike the ElevenLabs reel (Bear's own
   emails) or the watermark reel (Anthropic's own post), nothing here is a primary
   document held in hand. The reel's claims should be checked against statute text and
   official registers before it ships.
2. **The two supplied documents disagree in places.** Where they conflict, the reel
   follows the more careful one and states the narrower claim.

## Claims the reel makes, and what backs them

| # | Claim in narration | Status |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | No general US federal statute requires commercial providers to watermark | Both docs agree. EO 14110 was agency-directed and revoked; S.2765 died in the 118th Congress; the REAL Act covers federal officials only |

| 5 | EU reach is tied to EU-market placement or output used in the Union | Both docs agree; neither reads it as worldwide |

| 7 | China, India, South Korea have independent live regimes | Both docs agree; details differ, so the reel states only that they exist and differ |

## Corrections applied during authoring

1. **"California only requires marking for Californians" — CUT.** Doc 1 flags this as
   too categorical: the statute's operative command is not textually limited to files
   generated for users in California. The reel never makes the geofence claim.
2. **"Identical August 2 deadline" — SOFTENED.** The EU transition to December 2026 for
   incumbent systems means the dates are not the same hard technical deadline.
3. **Commercial motive — NOT ASSERTED.** The reel says the choice was a company's, and
   flags the cheap-pipeline reason as an inference. It never claims to know why.
