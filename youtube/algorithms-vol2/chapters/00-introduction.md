# Introduction

A learner opens the first chapter of *Algorithms, Vol. 2: with LLMs* with a familiar problem: there is too much information and not enough structure. The terms are available. The examples are available. The missing thing is a route through the material that turns exposure into understanding.

## What This Book Is

This book is a structured introduction to Algorithms, Vol. 2: with LLMs. It teaches the vocabulary of the field, shows how the main ideas connect, and gives readers enough conceptual grip to continue with more specialized work. This book is not a substitute for practice, mentorship, experimentation, or domain-specific judgment. It does not try to say everything. It tries to say enough, in the right order, so that the reader can recognize what matters next.

## The Concept Running Through the Book
