## Claims and verification

1. **Below a threshold frequency, no electrons — ever, at any intensity.**
   Source: Millikan (1914–1916), Phys. Rev. 7, 355. VERIFIED in FACTCHECK.md.

2. **Above threshold, electrons emerge immediately, with no time delay.**
   Source: same Millikan series. VERIFIED in FACTCHECK.md.
   (Measured emission delay < 10⁻⁹ s even at minimum intensity — classical wave
   theory would predict minutes-to-hours for sub-threshold dimming.)

3. **The kinetic energy of each electron depends only on frequency, not intensity.**
   Source: same Millikan series; Einstein 1905 prediction. VERIFIED in FACTCHECK.md.
