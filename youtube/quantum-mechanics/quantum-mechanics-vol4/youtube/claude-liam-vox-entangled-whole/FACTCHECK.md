# Fact check

- For `|Phi+> = (|00> + |11>)/sqrt(2)`, the joint density matrix is pure and has purity 1.
- Either one-qubit reduced state is `I/2`, has zero Bloch vector, and purity 1/2.
- Every one-qubit projective measurement is locally unbiased, but joint correlations depend on axes and on the Bell state.
- For `|Phi+>`, equal-axis correlations are +1 for x and z and -1 for y.
- Partial trace is a mathematical reduction, not a measurement.
- A pure bipartite state's subsystem is mixed exactly when the joint state is entangled.

The script omits the card's excluded matrix-by-matrix partial-trace calculation and Schmidt-decomposition detour.
