# Factcheck

VERDICT: PASS

- Decoherence is described as entanglement with environmental degrees of freedom and suppression of locally observable interference.
- The script avoids presenting a single universal cat decoherence time; it states the dependence on size, separation, temperature, and coupling.
- Approximate diagonalization in an interaction-selected pointer basis is separated from literal collapse.
- Decoherence addresses the preferred-basis and interference problem but does not by itself select a unique outcome.
- No faster-than-light, observer-causes-collapse, or interpretation-specific claim is made.
