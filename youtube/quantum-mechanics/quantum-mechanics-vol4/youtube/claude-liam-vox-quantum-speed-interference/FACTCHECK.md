# Factcheck

VERDICT: PASS

- Superposition is described as one coherent state with amplitudes over basis states, not readable classical parallelism.
- Immediate computational-basis measurement returns one sampled outcome with Born-rule probabilities.
- Quantum algorithms obtain useful information by arranging relative phases and interference before measurement.
- Constructive and destructive interference operate on amplitudes before probabilities are computed.
- Interference alone is not claimed to guarantee speedup; exploitable problem structure and a suitable circuit are required.
- The script avoids excluded Deutsch gate algebra, phase-kickback derivation, and Grover/Shor specifics.
