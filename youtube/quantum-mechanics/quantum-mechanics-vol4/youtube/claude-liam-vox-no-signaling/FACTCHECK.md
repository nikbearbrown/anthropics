# Factcheck

VERDICT: PASS

- Bob's local marginal for a Bell pair is maximally mixed and independent of Alice's measurement basis when her outcome is unavailable.
- Alice's Z and X measurements produce different conditional ensembles but the same unconditioned reduced state for Bob.
- The invariance statement is correctly scoped to local trace-preserving operations with outcomes ignored.
- Postselection can alter a conditional ensemble but identifying selected runs requires an ordinary classical message.
- Bell correlations are observable only after records are compared; no faster-than-light detector-count change is claimed.
- Reduced-density-matrix algebra, no-cloning, and signaling-loophole formalism are excluded.
