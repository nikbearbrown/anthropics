# Factcheck

VERDICT: PASS

- Below-threshold suppression is stated conditionally for a code, decoder, syndrome circuit, and noise model, not as a universal hardware percentage.
- Increasing distance raises the minimum weight of a logical operator while also increasing hardware and circuit overhead.
- Curve crossing is presented as an empirical threshold region, not proof from one device-level error number.
- Correlated noise, leakage, drift, and crosstalk are identified as threats to simplified threshold assumptions.
- Fault-tolerant syndrome extraction and logical operations remain required; encoding alone is not claimed to suffice.
- No dated device-specific performance claim is used, and surface-code stabilizer detail and scaling-exponent formulas are excluded.
