# Factcheck

VERDICT: PASS

- Ion motion is described as a set of collective normal modes, not one universal spring belonging only to the center-of-mass mode.
- State-dependent optical forces mediate an effective spin-spin interaction through motional dynamics.
- A clean gate is correctly scoped to closing the motional trajectory and disentangling motion from spin while retaining a conditional spin phase.
- Flexible long-range connectivity is not equated with identical, perfect, zero-cost all-to-all gates.
- Unsupported fixed 20-ion, 190-interaction, 570-SWAP, and zero-routing claims are removed.
- Pair-dependent participation, pulse shaping, heating, chain size, and crosstalk limits are explicit.
