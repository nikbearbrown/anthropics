# QUESTION

**The question:** "Why is a watermark changing a word supposed to be harmless?"

**Asked by:** a reader who had followed the earlier explanation of how marking works
and got stuck on exactly the right step.

**Why it earns a reel:** the answer is an assumption, not a result — and the
assumption has a two-word counterexample anyone can check. It's also the rare case
where the counterexample is more memorable than the claim it defeats.

> "Someone asked why a watermark changing a word is supposed to be harmless. The
> assumption underneath is worth checking. Liam. Take them through it."
