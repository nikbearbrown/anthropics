# QUESTION

**The question:** "If these companies can build a good AI detector, why is the one I
can actually use so bad?"

**Asked by:** a teacher — the question comes overwhelmingly from people who have to
act on a detector's output.

**Why it earns a reel:** the intuitive answer is a conspiracy, and the real answer is
an incentive structure that produces the same outcome without anyone deciding to be
dishonest. That's both more defensible and more useful, because it tells you what
would actually fix it.

**Host line (B00):**

> "Someone asked why AI detectors stay bad when the companies say they could do
> better. The answer is structural. Liam. Take them through it."
