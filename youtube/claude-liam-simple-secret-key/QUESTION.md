# QUESTION

**The question:** "If a detector says AI wrote this, how do I check that it's right?"

**Asked by:** a reader — the question arrives most often from teachers and editors,
i.e. the people who have to act on the answer.

**Why it earns a reel:** the answer is "you can't," and almost nobody expects that,
because "watermark" and "cryptographic" carry an implied promise of verifiability
that this design cannot keep. The gap between the expected answer and the real one is
where the damage lands — in hearings, newsrooms, and courtrooms.

**Host line (B00):**

> "Someone asked how you check whether a watermark detector is telling the truth.
> Turns out you can't. Liam. Take them through it."
