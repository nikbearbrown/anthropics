This factcheck applies the following verdicts:
- **STRUCTURAL** — general claim supported by the known structure of these products; would require active disproof to be wrong
- **PLAUSIBLE** — consistent with product direction known from training data; specific figures not confirmed
- **UNCONFIRMED** — specific 2026 figure or claim from source only; source's own citations unresolved; must be verified at vendor console before use
- **CONTRADICTED** — two or more sources give incompatible figures for the same claim; excluded from on-screen evidence
- **EXCLUDED** — claim stripped from the reel because it cannot be confirmed and is too specific to label carefully

On-screen treatment: UNCONFIRMED figures appear only if labeled "(unconfirmed — verify at console, August 2026)" or are compressed to a structural claim (e.g., "tens of thousands" not "14,400").
