# FACTCHECK — show-tell-a-model-corrects-itself

**What this is.** Every claim the film speaks or shows, checked against its named sources: the Constitutional AI paper, `anthropics/claude-cookbooks/misc/data/Constitutional AI.pdf` (arXiv 2212.08073v1, 15 Dec 2022, Bai et al.; author line "Anthropic"; sha256 `9a456a07…98e83`; all 34 pages extracted with PyMuPDF on 2026-09-27 and read from the raw text, saved as `sources/constitutional-ai-2212.08073v1.txt`; no summary was used), and the paper's repo files `anthropics/ConstitutionalHarmlessnessPaper/prompts/CritiqueRevisionInstructions.json` (16 critique/revision pairs, `harmful0`–`harmful15`) and `RLMadisonInstructions.json` (16 comparison principles). The repo README is one line, so the paper is the source for every claim about the method. **Result:** PASS, with two points CORRECTED. (1) The paper is inconsistent about which model is fine-tuned in stage one: the abstract says "finetune the original model on revised responses", while §1.2, §3.1 and §3.2 say a *pre-trained* model is fine-tuned on the revisions. The film says "a model is fine-tuned", which is true under both readings. (2) Stage two is not "AI feedback only": the paper's preference model is a *hybrid*, trained on AI labels for harmlessness mixed with **human** labels for helpfulness (§1.2, §4.1), and the film says so (B09). Every number spoken is the paper's own (16 principles, 4 revisions per prompt, 2022) and is attributed to the paper. The film says aloud (B12) that this is the 2022 method in the paper, not a claim about how any model is trained today. Drawings that are not data have EXEMPT rows.
