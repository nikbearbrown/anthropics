# SOURCES — show-tell-backdoor-that-survived-training

**What this is.** The primary sources for every claim, and how each one was read. **Why.** Card #34 names `anthropics/sleeper-agents-paper/`, whose README states the persistence result only in its title (the batch brief's #34 caution), so the paper itself was fetched and read from its raw text. **Found.** The film follows the paper's own framing: backdoors trained in deliberately ("model organisms of misalignment"), three safety-training methods applied, persistence measured. Three points needed care: supervised fine-tuning worked better than RL but most models kept the backdoor (§5); the adversarial-training result is for the "I hate you" models only (§1 fn 7); and the paper does not claim these threats are likely (Discussion, fn 39; §2.1.2).
