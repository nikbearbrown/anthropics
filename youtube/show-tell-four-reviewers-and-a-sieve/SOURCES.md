The README's CI/CD notes, best practices, troubleshooting and author line; the command's exact comment template and the "react with 👍" footer; the example link format beyond "the full commit hash". Nothing is averaged in this plugin: each issue gets one score from its own Haiku agent, and the film says so without mentioning averages. The sibling film "Seven Steps Before a Feature Ships" shows feature-dev's `code-reviewer` agent with its own ≥ 80 rule; this film states only this plugin's files. The scores on screen (90, 100, 75, 25, 50, 0) are illustrations, not output from a run.
