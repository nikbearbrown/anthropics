### B01 — STILL · archive

**Beat:** COLD OPEN, Hiroshima aftermath photograph.

**Public-archive links:**
- NARA (US National Archives): https://catalog.archives.gov/search?q=hiroshima+1945&f.type=image
  - Look for US Army Air Forces aerial/elevated photographs, August–September 1945, public domain
  - Record group 243 (US Strategic Bombing Survey) is the primary source
- Wikimedia Commons: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Hiroshima_after_bombing
  - Multiple PD photographs available; prefer wide/elevated view showing scale of destruction

**Provenance notes:** US government photographs taken by military personnel are public domain (17 USC 105). Credit line: "US Army Air Forces / NARA" or source specific to the image.
