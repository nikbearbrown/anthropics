## What this reel adds

The source frames all four uses under one heading: *"the reassuring part is that you have control."* True, and it flattens a distinction that decides how careful a person should actually be. **The four differ in reversibility**, and the source never draws that axis:

| Use | Reversible? |
|---|---|
| 1 · the conversation | yes |
| 2 · product memory | yes |
| 3 · provider systems | partial — you can request, you don't set the clock |
| 4 · training | **no** |

The source's own best line — *"your detail isn't really your exact words anymore — it's a pattern"* — is offered as reassurance and is also the point of no return. Opt-out is prospective.
