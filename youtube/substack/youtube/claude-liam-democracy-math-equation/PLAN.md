## THE EPISTEMIC FRAME (governs the whole build — hard rule 5)

The essay is a METHODS piece wearing a findings costume. It mixes:
- **(R) Reported events** — the Wyden/Van Hollen letter; exclusion portals suspended
  Feb 2025; the Nov 4 Swiss delegation (Rolex desk clock, $130k gold bar "45 and 47");
  the Nov Swiss tariff cut 39%→15%; Apple's $600B pledge + gold-base plaque; ballroom
  donors; the 2018–2020 Section 301 research (~3.94pp per SD of contributions).
- **(M) The method** — Bayesian hierarchical logit, priors, Bayes Factors, changepoint
  detection, event studies. Real statistics, correctly described.
- **(I) Illustrative computed results** — posterior 6.2pp, ballroom 14.7pp, BF = 247,
  91% gift-to-reversal, $43B Q1 savings. These are the essay's WORKED SCENARIO, not a
  published study. The reel presents them as "here is what the analysis would return
  IF the data behave as the essay sketches" — never as established findings.

The reel's own thesis (on-brand for skepticism.ai): the interesting thing is not the
accusation — it's the machinery that could settle it, and what that machinery can and
cannot prove. The falsifiability act carries the essay's own caveats forward.
