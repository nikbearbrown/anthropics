# Spending Your Effort

**What this is.** A 150-second show-tell explainer of Claude's effort setting, built around one hero object: a kraft effort dial with five marked levels (low, medium, high, xhigh, max). It has labels only, and Liam's voice does the explaining. **Why.** Bear asked for it on 2026-09-26 ("show-tell for this film as well") with 16 frames of Anthropic's effort-dial animation (card #9 in `../show-tell-ideas.md`). **Found.** Every claim matches Thariq Shihipar's "Using Claude Code: Spending your effort" (claude.dev, Sep 25, 2026) or Anthropic's effort docs. Effort is "an approximation of how much compute you want it to spend on the task". Higher effort buys more independent verification and judgement, like the gap between a one-hour and a twelve-hour deadline. On html-js-filter (a Terminal-Bench 3.0 HTML sanitizer), Fable 5.1 passed 1 of 5 tries at low (about 2 minutes, one pass, one hand-written test page) and 5 of 5 at **xhigh**. The traced run took about 33 minutes: it reviewed its own draft, read the parser's source, ran an XSS test suite and wrote a fuzzer. Then come his rule of thumb per level and his loop (interview → build on low → review → verify on high), with `/effort` to switch mid-conversation. Two corrections (see FACTCHECK): the 5/5 run is xhigh, not high; and the 33-minute traced run is left unlabelled, because the article calls it "a high-effort run" and says the worked examples may use intermediate settings.

- The beats: the dial (B00); what effort is, compute (B01); one hour against twelve hours (B02); verification and judgement (B03); html-js-filter at low (B04) and at xhigh (B05); low and medium (B06); high and max (B07); the loop and `/effort` (B08).
