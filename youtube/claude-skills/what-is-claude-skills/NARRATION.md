# NARRATION — what-is-claude-skills

## B00 (cold open)
Jambo, Bear. This channel runs on computational skepticism, which means no claim gets a pass for where it came from. A skill that nobody has tried to break isn't validated. It's just unrefuted. Here's how to judge a skill somebody else wrote before you trust it.

## B01 (wrong model)
The wrong model: installing a skill by name and assuming it does what the title says. The name says 'summarizer.' You don't read what it actually instructs. It deletes source files.

## B02 (right model)
The right model: open the SKILL.md and read what it actually instructs — the constraints, what it bans, the assumptions it makes about your setup. The name is marketing. The instructions are the contract.

## B03 (what's in the playlist)
The playlist covers where skills come from, how to read one critically, when to adapt versus fork, and what a bad skill looks like from the outside.

## B04 (do now)
Open a skill you already use and read its instructions end to end. Not the README. Not the name. The instructions.

## B05 (verdict)
Who this is for: anyone whose skill library grew faster than their understanding of it. Skip if you only run skills you wrote yourself. Note: this playlist and the agent-skills playlist are deliberately split — writing your own versus judging someone else's. If that split doesn't hold, this is the reel to rebuild. Falsifiable line: if a skill's stated description accurately predicts its behavior in every case, reading the SKILL.md adds no value.

## B06 (handoff)
Your turn. Read this SKILL.md as a skeptic. What will it do that I wouldn't expect from the name, what does it assume about my setup, and where would it do a bad job?
